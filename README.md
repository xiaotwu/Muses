**Polyhymnia** is the macOS edition and the heart of the [Muses family](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses). The application is named **Muses**: a native home for music, playlists, videos and podcasts, designed around artwork and a thoughtful listening flow.

[![Muses Home on macOS](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/assets/tour-home.png)](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/#tour)

## Listen your way

- Browse Home, songs, albums, artists, music videos and playlists.
- Keep favorites, pins and listening history together in your library.
- Play from a queue that remembers your collection, with separate **Up Next**, repeat, shuffle and Smart Shuffle.
- Open **Now Playing** for cover or vinyl artwork and available lyrics, or watch a video on demand.
- Follow podcasts, resume episodes, adjust speed and skip back or forward.
- Control playback from the floating player, menu bar, keyboard shortcuts and system media controls.

## Made for the Mac

An artwork-led library, an integrated queue and a floating player keep browsing and listening connected. Native desktop controls, adaptable appearance and an immersive Now Playing view give your music room without getting in the way.

## Get Muses

Requires **macOS 14 or later**. Download the signed, notarized application from [Releases](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases/tag/polyhymnia%2Fv0.5.6) and follow the [installation guide](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/installation.html).
