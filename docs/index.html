A library with context.

Bring in YouTube playlists. Keep favorites, listening history and a queue that remembers where you were.

Room for the song.

Large artwork, cover and vinyl modes, source-matched lyrics and colors drawn from the music’s cover.

At home on macOS.

Glass capsule controls, keyboard navigation, system media controls and a compact menu-bar player.

Less searching. More clarity.

Settings, opened up.

One sidebar. Clear sections. Adaptive light and dark appearances, with native glass where your Mac supports it.

Independent. Local. In progress.

Your library lives on your Mac. Personalized Home is optional, separately authorized and read-only. Muses contains no analytics SDK.

This preview includes public playback and browsing. Complete catalog relationships, podcasts, production account writes and the full hardware matrix are still in development. The public build has no Google OAuth client configured. Translation and Intelligence depend on your Mac and platform availability.
