# Muses 0.5.1

The first public release of Muses is ready for macOS 14 and later. Download the signed, notarized app from the DMG and drag it into Applications.

## Highlights

- Browse your music and playlists through an artwork-led Home, with favorites, history, and a queue that keeps its context.
- Enjoy a floating player, full-screen Now Playing, lyrics, menu-bar controls, and system media controls.
- Follow shows, browse episodes, resume listening, adjust speed, and skip 15 seconds.
- Explore short videos from followed channels and read comments and replies in a centered video panel. Comments are read-only in Muses.
- Connect an account for subscriptions and an optional personal Home.
- Keep your existing library when upgrading from an earlier preview.

The remaining platform and long-running checks are recorded in [Remaining validation](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Muses-Polyhymnia/blob/main/docs/remaining-validation-0.5.1.md).
